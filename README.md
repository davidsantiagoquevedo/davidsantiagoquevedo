
## Hola 👋

I'm David, a physicist with expertise in computational, mathematical, and data-driven modelling of complex systems. My expertise spans nonequilibrium statistical physics and stochastic processes, epidemiology and public health, and wealth inequality. I'm currently finishing my PhD at the [Institute for Theoretical Physics](https://www.uu.nl/en/research/institute-for-theoretical-physics) at Utrecht University, the Netherlands.

🔭 **My PhD research** focuses on the study of non-Markovian stochastic processes, combining tools from fractional calculus, stochastic field theories, and numerical analysis. My goal is to describe physical systems where the interaction between anomalous diffusion and nonequilibrium statistical physics plays a central role.

**Some of my projects in theoretical physics:**

- [Dynamics of the fractional Langevin equations coupled to in- and out-of-equilibrium reservoirs](https://github.com/davidsantiagoquevedo/fractional-langevin-equations)
- [Library to simulate and analyse fractional Brownian motions](https://github.com/davidsantiagoquevedo/fractional-brownian-motion)
- [Package-like implementation of generalized Mittag-Leffler functions](https://github.com/davidsantiagoquevedo/mittag_leffler)
- [Molecular dynamics simulation of Brownian motion in a gas](https://github.com/davidsantiagoquevedo/brownian-motion)

🦠 **My research in epidemiology** focuses on building mathematical and computational models to project epidemic scenarios and estimate epidemiological parameters. During the COVID-19 pandemic (2020–2022), I supported the Colombian government's response through mathematical modelling and real-time epidemiological data analysis. Later, between 2022 and 2024, I contributed to the pandemic preparedness initiative [Epiverse-Trace](https://epiverse-trace.github.io/) as a Research Software Engineer.

**Some of my projects in epidemiology:**

- [vaccineff: an R package with tools for estimating vaccine effectiveness](https://github.com/epiverse-trace/vaccineff)
- [Transmission and severity analysis of the four waves of SARS-CoV-2 in Bogotá](https://github.com/TRACE-LAC/covid19-waves-bogota)
- [Compartmental model used by the Colombian government to build the detect-isolate-and-trace strategy for COVID-19 (DAR/PRASS)](https://github.com/davidsantiagoquevedo/SEIR-DIT)
